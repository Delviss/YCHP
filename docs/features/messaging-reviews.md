# Messaging, Reviews & Notifications

Reviews, messaging and notifications are part of the Phase 1 launch.

---

## Messaging

| Who | Can |
|---|---|
| **Travellers** | Message a property |
| **Property owners** | Message guests |

---

## Reviews

- Travellers can **review a completed stay**.
- Property owners can **reply to reviews**.
- Ratings and reviews are shown on search results and property pages.

---

## Notifications

Travellers receive notifications about their bookings and trips, alongside a confirmation and receipt for each booking.

!!! note "Calm, not urgent"
    Notifications and copy avoid countdown timers and "only 2 rooms left" pressure tactics. See [Voice & Tone](../brand/voice-tone.md).
