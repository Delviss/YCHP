# Curation

Curation is Yana's first pillar: **recommended stays and deals chosen for the traveller**, in the calm, premium style of Luxury Escapes.

---

## Why Curation

Existing booking sites are busy and one-size-fits-all. For travellers who have never booked this kind of trip, an endless list is intimidating. Yana offers a **considered shortlist** instead, deliberately avoiding the crowded style of Agoda and Trip.com.

---

## The Curation Tab

The **Curation** tab is a short request form for travellers who want **YCHP-style end-to-end help** rather than a self-serve booking journey. The Yana team uses it to put together recommendations suited to that traveller.

---

## Curated Picks Across the Platform

- **Curated deals** are presented by the Yana team from launch.
- **Curated Pick** badges highlight recommended options in search results.
- The **AI travel assistant** (Phase 2) will suggest curated options, help people plan and answer questions, and learn from its user across future trips. See [Coming After Launch](coming-after-launch.md).
