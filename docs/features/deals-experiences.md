# Deals & Experiences

Offers on Yana go **beyond the hotel room**, so a booking can be a full experience, not just a bed for the night.

---

## Deals

Deals are **curated and presented by the Yana team**: offers that combine price, inclusions and timing into something genuinely worth noticing, not a wall of discounts. They cover:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Stays** | Hand-picked hotel and property offers |
| **Dining** | Restaurant experiences |
| **Spa** | Spa treatments |
| **Activities** | Things to do at the destination |

Property owners can also run **their own promotions**, and the Yana team runs **platform-wide promotions**.

---

## Experiences

The **Experiences** tab covers dining, spa, tours, activities and other add-ons that can turn a room booking into a full trip. **Deeper activity inventory** arrives in [Phase 2](../phases.md).

---

## Merchandising Without Clutter

Destination-based and retargeted promotions should feel **editorial and relevant**. Advertising can exist inside Yana, but it should never make the interface feel like a discount marketplace.
