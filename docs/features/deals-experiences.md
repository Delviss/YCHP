# Deals & Experiences

Offers on YCHP go **beyond the hotel room**, so a booking can be a full experience, not just a bed for the night.

---

## Deals

Deals are **curated and presented by the YCHP team** and cover:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Stays** | Hand-picked hotel and property offers |
| **Dining** | Restaurant experiences |
| **Spa** | Spa treatments |
| **Activities** | Things to do at the destination |

Property owners can also run **their own promotions**, and the YCHP team runs **platform-wide promotions**.

---

## Experiences

The **Experiences** tab covers activities, spa and tours. The wider **activities and attractions** catalogue arrives in [Phase 2](../phases.md).
