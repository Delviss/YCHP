# Coming After Launch

Phase 1 is a complete, working platform on its own. These features follow soon after launch or as YCHP grows. See [Phases](../phases.md).

---

## Phase 2 — Soon After Launch

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Group Savings (Stokvel)** | Saving toward one trip as a family or group. See [Group Savings](../wallet/group-savings.md) |
| **AI travel assistant** | Guided, curated planning, with the ability to know its user and learn from them across future trips and interactions |
| **Flights** | Shown in the Skyscanner style, linking out to the airline or agent |
| **Activities and attractions** | A wider catalogue of things to do |

---

## Phase 3 — Growth

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Car hire** | Rent a car as part of the trip |
| **Homes and apartments** | A wider range of places to stay |
| **More markets** | Additional markets, currencies and languages |

---

## Last Phase

- **Gift cards** and **cruises** (inspired by Scoopon AU)
- **"Buy now, choose dates later"**, still being worked out with partners

## Targeted Ads

Retargeting and destination-based targeting are wanted, but must be integrated **without cluttering the experience**.
