# Launch Tabs

Five tabs sit at the centre of Yana at launch. The core navigation should be simple and immediately understandable.

---

| Tab | What it does | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Hotels** | Search and browse a considered range of stays with strong photography, transparent pricing, availability, policies, maps and relevant filters | [Search & Discovery](search-discovery.md), [Property Pages](property-pages.md), [Booking & Payments](booking-payments.md) |
| **Deals** | Yana-curated offers that combine price, inclusions and timing into something genuinely worth noticing, not a wall of discounts | [Deals & Experiences](deals-experiences.md) |
| **Curation** | A short request form for travellers who want YCHP-style end-to-end help rather than a self-serve booking journey | [Curation](curation.md) |
| **Experiences** | Dining, spa, tours, activities and other add-ons that turn a room booking into a full trip | [Deals & Experiences](deals-experiences.md) |
| **Wallet** | The financial planning layer: save personally, contribute as a group, see progress and move from intention to booking | [Wallet](../wallet/index.md) |

---
