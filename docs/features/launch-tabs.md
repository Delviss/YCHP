# Launch Tabs

Five tabs were confirmed as key to the initial launch. Together they cover the three pillars of YCHP: **curation**, **deals beyond the room** and **saving for the trip**.

---

| Tab | What it does | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Hotels** | Search, compare and book places to stay: the heart of the platform at launch | [Search & Discovery](search-discovery.md), [Property Pages](property-pages.md), [Booking & Payments](booking-payments.md) |
| **Deals** | Curated offers presented by the YCHP team, covering stays as well as dining, spa and activities | [Deals & Experiences](deals-experiences.md) |
| **Curation** | A short form to request a curated experience | [Curation](curation.md) |
| **Experiences** | Activities, spa and tours | [Deals & Experiences](deals-experiences.md) |
| **Wallet** | Save money toward a trip over time, alone or as a group | [Wallet](../wallet/index.md) |

---
