# Property Pages

Opening a result leads to a property page that gives a traveller everything needed to decide with confidence.

---

## What a Property Page Shows

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Photos** | The property and its rooms |
| **Description** | What the place is and what makes it special |
| **Map** | Location and surroundings |
| **Amenities** | Facilities available at the property |
| **Rules** | House rules |
| **Check-in / check-out** | Times for arrival and departure |
| **Cancellation policy** | What happens if plans change |
| **Available rooms** | Rooms, availability and pricing for the chosen dates |

---

## Verification Badge

Properties are verified by the YCHP team before they go live and carry a verification badge. See [Property Verification](../trust-verification.md).

---

## Keeping Availability Accurate

Availability comes through a **channel manager** connection rather than individual integrations with each property system, keeping it accurate across tens of thousands of rooms.
