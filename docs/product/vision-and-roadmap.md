# YCHP — Product Vision & Roadmap

*Condensed from "YCHP – Product Vision & Roadmap", v1.0, 17 September 2026.*

This is the plain-language reference for what YCHP is, why it's worth building, who it's for, and the current plan for when each part becomes ready. It intentionally stays free of technical detail — see the companion **Product Requirements Document** (every feature, in full) and **Technical Requirements Document** (the build approach for web and mobile) for that.

## 1. The vision, in one page

**Your Curated Holiday Planner is a travel booking platform that makes a great holiday feel reachable.** Instead of a long, cluttered list of rooms, YCHP hand-picks stays and deals, lets people save toward a trip over time, and guides them from the first idea to a confirmed booking.

At launch, the heart of the platform is **hotels and other places to stay**. Around that core, YCHP adds three things ordinary booking sites don't offer together:

- **Curation, not clutter** — recommended stays and deals chosen for the traveller, in the calm, premium style of Luxury Escapes.
- **Deals beyond the room** — dining, spa treatments and activities, so a booking can be a full experience, not just a bed for the night.
- **A way to save for the trip** — a built-in wallet where a person, or a group of people together, can put money aside toward a holiday over time.

Flights (shown Skyscanner-style, linking out to the airline or agent), car hire, activities and an AI travel assistant follow soon after launch.

> **In one sentence:** YCHP turns "I could never afford that" into "I have been saving, and it is booked."

**Launch tabs (confirmed as key to initial launch):** Hotels · Deals · Curation (a short form to request a curated experience) · Experiences (activities, spa, tours) · Wallet.

## 2. Why this platform matters

### 2.1 The problem

There is a large and growing group of middle- and upper-class Africans who can afford to travel internationally but haven't done so yet. Many assume travel is out of reach. The process feels intimidating, and they rarely see people like themselves travelling — so it doesn't feel like something "for them."

The barrier isn't only money. It's confidence and familiarity. Existing booking sites make this worse: busy, one-size-fits-all, and asking people to pay all at once for something that feels far away.

### 2.2 The opportunity

YCHP removes the fear and shows a great trip is achievable — by curating choices so people aren't overwhelmed, presenting travel in an aspirational but welcoming way, and letting people save toward a trip at their own pace, alone or with family and friends. This is a market the big platforms aren't speaking to directly.

### 2.3 Who it serves

| Group | What they want | How YCHP helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Travellers** | A trip they can trust and afford, without feeling lost | Curated stays and deals, clear pricing, a wallet to save toward the trip, and guidance along the way |
| **Property owners** | More bookings and simple tools to manage them | A profile, room and price control, a booking calendar, payouts and a clear dashboard |
| **The YCHP team** | Control of quality, money and disputes | An admin area to verify properties, manage bookings and payments, run promotions and help resolve issues |

## 3. What makes YCHP different

Five things set YCHP apart from Booking.com, Agoda and similar sites:

1. **Curated, not cluttered** — every search leads to a considered set of options, not an endless list. Calm, premium look and feel, inspired by Luxury Escapes; deliberately avoiding the busy, crowded style of Agoda and Trip.com.
2. **Deals beyond the hotel room** — offers cover dining, spa treatments and activities as well as stays.
3. **Save toward a trip, alone or together** — a built-in wallet lets a traveller set money aside over time, and a group can save together toward one trip, in the spirit of a stokvel. Strong fit for the audience; something mainstream sites don't offer.
4. **A guided, human feel** — an AI travel assistant (arriving shortly after launch) helps people plan, suggests curated options and answers questions, so first-time travellers never feel on their own.
5. **Built for the region** — local payment methods through Paystack, prices shown in the traveller's currency, support in English and French from day one.

## 4. What YCHP will do

### 4.1 For travellers

- Create an account and manage their profile and past trips.
- Search for stays by city, country, region, hotel name, landmark, airport or map, with flexible dates.
- Filter by price, rating, property type, distance, facilities (Wi-Fi, parking, pool, gym, breakfast, restaurant) and more.
- See clear results: photos, price, total price, taxes and fees, rating and reviews, distance and extras such as free airport transfers.
- Open a property page with photos, description, map, amenities, rules, check-in/check-out times, cancellation policy and available rooms.
- Book a room — including on behalf of someone else — with a clear summary before paying.
- Pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, at the property, or pay later, and receive a confirmation and receipt.
- Save money into a wallet and check the balance at any time, alone or as a group.
- Message a property, review a completed stay, cancel or request a refund, and receive notifications.

### 4.2 For property owners

- Create an account and list a property with photos, rooms and prices.
- Set availability, block dates, change prices by date and offer discounts.
- Receive and manage bookings, message guests and reply to reviews.
- See revenue and payouts, run their own promotions, and work from a clear dashboard.

### 4.3 For the YCHP team

- Verify properties before they go live and award a verification badge.
- Manage customers, properties, bookings, payments, refunds and disputes.
- Run platform-wide promotions and read reports.
- Oversee the wallet and group savings ("Stokvel") area and, later, the AI travel assistant.

### 4.4 Product notes to carry forward

- **Wallet / group saving model:** look at a Tricount-style split — when multiple people save toward one trip, the cost splits automatically and everyone can see how much they need to save and how much they've each contributed.
- **Targeted ads:** retargeting and destination-based targeting are wanted, but must be integrated without cluttering the experience.
- **Inspiration for later phases:** Scoopon AU (Hotels & Resorts) — gift cards and cruises to be included in the last phase.
- **"Buy now, choose dates later"** is still being worked out with partners — planned for the last phase to allow time to iron out the details.

## 5. What's ready at launch, and what comes later

The team would rather move the launch date than launch with important features missing. Work is grouped into three phases — **Phase 1 is a complete, working platform on its own**; Phases 2 and 3 add to it.

### Phase 1 — Launch

A complete place-to-stay marketplace, working end to end on website, iPhone and Android:

- Accounts for travellers, property owners and the YCHP team.
- Search, filters, map, property pages, rooms, availability and pricing.
- Booking, payments, invoices, cancellations and refunds.
- Reviews, messaging, notifications and the property dashboard.
- Property verification, promotions and the admin area.
- The individual Savings Wallet, and curated deals presented by the YCHP team.

### Phase 2 — Soon after launch

- **Group Savings (Stokvel)** — saving toward one trip as a family or group. *(Flagged as a strong selling point, ideally in Phase 1 — timing to be confirmed if it stays in Phase 2.)*
- **AI travel assistant** for guided, curated planning — with the ability to know its user and learn from them across future trips and interactions.
- **Flights**, shown in the Skyscanner style, linking out to the airline or agent.
- **Activities and attractions.**

### Phase 3 — Growth

- Car hire.
- Wider range of homes and apartments.
- More markets, currencies and languages.

**Why phase it this way:** launching the stays marketplace first gets YCHP earning and learning from real travellers quickly. Flights, activities and the AI assistant are valuable, but depend on the core being solid first — phasing protects both quality and the launch date.

## 6. Roadmap

An indicative plan, measured in weeks from the day the build starts, assuming it begins with design and confirming the open points in Section 7 below.

| Stage | Roughly how long | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery & design | Weeks 1–4 | Confirm scope and open points; design the traveller, owner and admin experiences in the luxury style |
| Foundation | Weeks 3–6 | Shared groundwork for web and mobile, sign-in, and payment setup |
| Core build | Weeks 5–16 | Search, property pages, booking, payments, wallet, dashboards and admin, built in working slices |
| Property onboarding | Weeks 5–16 | Bring properties on board and keep availability accurate, running alongside the build |
| Testing & trial | Weeks 14–19 | Full testing, a trial with a small group, and fixes |
| Launch | Around week 18–20 | Website and Android first, iPhone shortly after |

**Timing decision:** a full three-platform launch (website, iPhone, Android) with the complete Phase 1 feature set and a large property catalogue is realistic in the **February** window, not early December — and the team confirmed they're happy planning around the February date rather than rushing. If an earlier date mattered for a business reason, the fallback would be a website-first soft launch of the core booking journey to an invited group, with apps and remaining Phase 1 features following into the new year.

## 7. Open points — decided and outstanding

A few points needed a decision early because they shape cost, timing and how the platform works. None blocked the start of the build.

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | **Which markets come first** | Paid consumer acquisition at launch focuses on **Zimbabwe, South Africa, Zambia, Kenya, Nigeria and Ghana**, with organic demand captured globally over time. Initial supply is globally distributed, concentrated in **Asia and the Middle East** (strong travel appeal, competitive pricing, major hubs like Dubai), while the partner network actively onboards African properties pre-launch — building a core portfolio across Asia, the Middle East and Africa. Paystack (already in use) fits the African markets well. |
| 7.2 | **How commission is collected** | **Agreed:** automatic payment split through Paystack at the moment of payment, so the property receives its share and YCHP's ~15–20% commission is taken automatically. "Pay at the property" bookings, where no money passes through the platform, are invoiced to the property. This also simplifies refund processing. |
| 7.3 | **The savings wallet & group Stokvel** | The wallet is a key differentiator and needs regulatory care (holding customer savings can be regulated; group savings schemes have their own rules in some countries). **Decision:** partner with **First National Bank (FNB)**, which has an established framework for this wallet/savings model, and work with FNB's compliance team to handle legal and regulatory requirements before launch. |
| 7.4 | **One app or two** | **Agreed:** proceed with the recommended, most cost-effective approach — a single app that adapts to whether someone is a traveller or an owner, rather than two separate apps. |
| 7.5 | **Connecting to properties' existing systems** | **Agreed:** connect through a channel manager rather than integrating with each property system individually, to keep availability accurate across tens of thousands of rooms. Main systems in use will be reviewed during onboarding. |
| 7.6 | **Currency and languages** | **Confirmed:** prices shown in the traveller's local currency, with US Dollars and South African Rand required. Launch languages are **English and French**; the full currency list to be confirmed at launch. |

## 9. How the team works together

Work happens in short, visible steps rather than long stretches of silence:

1. Confirm scope and the open points above, then design the experience.
2. Build in working slices, with progress shown every couple of weeks.
3. Review and give feedback; adjust accordingly.
4. Test thoroughly, run a small trial, then launch and support the platform.

---

## 10. What success looks like

YCHP is doing its job when:

- A first-time traveller can go from idea to a confirmed, paid booking without feeling lost or intimidated.
- People are saving toward trips in the wallet, alone and in groups, and coming back.
- Property owners find the platform simple and see real bookings.
- The platform feels calm and premium, the way Luxury Escapes does — clearly not like the cluttered sites it deliberately avoids.
- The team can run the whole operation — money, quality and support — from the admin area with confidence.
