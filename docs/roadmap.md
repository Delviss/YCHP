# Roadmap

An indicative delivery shape, measured in **weeks from the day the build starts**.

---

## Timeline

| Stage | Indicative timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery + design** | Weeks 1–4 | Confirm scope, wallet and legal dependencies, and the end-to-end traveller, partner and admin journeys |
| **Foundation** | Weeks 3–6 | Shared web/mobile foundation, sign-in, payment setup and core data structures |
| **Core build** | Weeks 5–16 | Search, property pages, booking, wallet, group saving, experiences, dashboards and admin, in working slices |
| **Property onboarding** | Weeks 5–16 | Bring supply live and keep availability accurate, in parallel with the product build |
| **Testing + trial** | Weeks 14–19 | Full QA, a selected-user trial and refinement |
| **Launch** | Around weeks 18–20 | Target the **February** window rather than rush a December full-platform launch |

---

## Launch Dependencies

| Dependency | What needs to happen |
|---|---|
| **Wallet + legal** | Confirm the licensed partner structure, custody of funds and market-by-market compliance early |
| **Supply + availability** | Channel-manager integration and property onboarding must run in parallel with the build |
| **Trial group** | Use a small invited cohort to test discovery, saving, group contributions and booking before public launch |

---

## Timing Principle

!!! note "Trustworthy before fast"
    The product should launch when the **wallet, group saving and booking journey are trustworthy**. A rushed date is less valuable than a complete Yana experience.

See [Phases](phases.md) for what ships when.
