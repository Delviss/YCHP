# Roadmap

An indicative plan, measured in **weeks from the day the build starts**, assuming it begins with design and confirming the [open points](decisions.md).

---

## Timeline

| Stage | Roughly how long | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery & design** | Weeks 1–4 | Confirm scope and open points; design the traveller, owner and admin experiences in the luxury style |
| **Foundation** | Weeks 3–6 | Shared groundwork for web and mobile, sign-in, and payment setup |
| **Core build** | Weeks 5–16 | Search, property pages, booking, payments, wallet, dashboards and admin, built in working slices |
| **Property onboarding** | Weeks 5–16 | Bring properties on board and keep availability accurate, running alongside the build |
| **Testing & trial** | Weeks 14–19 | Full testing, a trial with a small group, and fixes |
| **Launch** | Around week 18–20 | Website and Android first, iPhone shortly after |

---

## Timing Decision

!!! note "February launch window"
    A full three-platform launch (website, iPhone, Android) with the complete Phase 1 feature set and a large property catalogue is realistic in the **February** window, not early December. The team confirmed they're happy planning around the February date rather than rushing.

**Fallback:** if an earlier date mattered for a business reason, the fallback would be a **website-first soft launch** of the core booking journey to an invited group, with apps and remaining Phase 1 features following into the new year.

See [Phases](phases.md) for what ships when.
