# Users & Access

Yana serves three groups, each with its own account type and tools.

---

## Roles

| Role | Account | Main tools |
|---|---|---|
| **Traveller** | Profile and past trips | Search, booking, wallet, messaging, reviews |
| **Property owner** | Property listing(s) | [Property Owner Dashboard](property-owners.md) |
| **Yana team** | Admin | [Yana Admin](admin.md) |

Accounts for all three roles are part of the **Phase 1** launch.

---

## One App, Not Two

!!! note "Decision"
    Yana uses the recommended, most cost-effective approach: **a single app that adapts to whether someone is a traveller or an owner**, rather than two separate apps.

The app runs on **website, iPhone and Android**.

---

## Traveller Accounts

Travellers can create an account and manage their profile and past trips, and can book on behalf of someone else. See [For Travellers](../getting-started/travellers.md).
