# Market & Opportunity

---

## The Problem

There is a large and growing group of **middle- and upper-class Africans** who can afford to travel internationally but haven't done so yet. Many assume travel is out of reach. The process feels intimidating, and they rarely see people like themselves travelling, so it doesn't feel like something "for them."

The barrier isn't only money. **It's confidence and familiarity.** Existing booking sites make this worse: busy, one-size-fits-all, and asking people to pay all at once for something that feels far away.

---

## The Opportunity

YCHP removes the fear and shows a great trip is achievable:

- **Curating choices** so people aren't overwhelmed
- Presenting travel in an **aspirational but welcoming** way
- Letting people **save toward a trip at their own pace**, alone or with family and friends

This is a market the big platforms aren't speaking to directly.

---

## Launch Markets

| Side | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Demand (travellers)** | Paid consumer acquisition at launch focuses on **Zimbabwe, South Africa, Zambia, Kenya, Nigeria and Ghana**, with organic demand captured globally over time |
| **Supply (properties)** | Globally distributed, concentrated in **Asia and the Middle East** (strong travel appeal, competitive pricing, major hubs like Dubai), while the partner network actively onboards **African properties** pre-launch |

The goal is a core portfolio across **Asia, the Middle East and Africa**. Paystack (already in use) fits the African markets well.
