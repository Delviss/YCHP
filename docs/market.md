# Market & Opportunity

**Travel aspiration is high. The journey to booking is still harder than it should be.**

---

## The Problem

Many travellers can afford to travel internationally over time, but the current booking experience assumes **confidence, familiarity and the ability to pay a large amount at once**. Mainstream platforms often add more choice when what people need is more confidence.

- Too much choice can make travel research feel like work rather than anticipation.
- A single large payment can make a premium holiday feel further away than it really is.
- Group trips are often coordinated through chats, spreadsheets and informal transfers, with little visibility over who has contributed what.
- First-time or less-frequent international travellers may need reassurance, not just inventory.

---

## The Opportunity

Yana brings **discovery, saving and booking into one calm experience**. It is built for **African travellers first**, while the destinations and product ambition are global. The brand should feel contemporary and universal, not themed around a single country, language or idea of what "Africa" looks like.

---

## Supply Strategy

Yana launches with a **globally distributed property portfolio**, with strong concentration across **Asia and the Middle East** and an expanding set of properties across **Africa**. These regions combine strong travel appeal, competitive accommodation pricing and major air hubs, while giving the platform room to merchandise both aspiration and value.

---

## Launch Demand Markets

Paid customer acquisition initially focuses on **Zimbabwe, South Africa, Zambia, Kenya, Nigeria and Ghana**, while the platform remains open to organic demand from elsewhere.

Prices display in the traveller's **local currency**, with **USD and ZAR** required from launch, and **English and French** supported from day one.
