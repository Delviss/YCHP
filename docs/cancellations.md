# Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellations, refunds and invoices are part of the **Phase 1** launch.

---

## For Travellers

- Every property page shows its **cancellation policy** before booking.
- Travellers can **cancel or request a refund** from their account.

---

## For the YCHP Team

The team manages **payments, refunds and disputes** from the [admin area](users-access/admin.md).

---

## Refunds and the Payment Split

Because commission is taken through an automatic **Paystack payment split** at the moment of payment, refund processing is simpler. See [Commission & Payments](commission.md).
