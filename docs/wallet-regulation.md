# Wallet Regulation

The Yana Wallet may hold customer funds, so it needs **regulatory care**.

---

## Why It Needs Care

- Holding customer savings **can be regulated**.
- Group savings schemes have **their own rules** in some countries.
- Each launch market needs its own review.

---

## Current Direction: a Licensed Partner

!!! note "Current direction"
    The wallet is built with a **licensed financial partner** and reviewed for each launch market. The current direction is to work with **First National Bank (FNB)**, which has an established framework for this wallet/savings model, and its compliance team. **Paystack** remains part of the payment infrastructure for bookings and commission flows.

---

## What Must Be Confirmed Early

The licensed partner structure, **custody of funds** and **market-by-market compliance** are launch dependencies, confirmed during discovery. See [Roadmap](roadmap.md).
