# Wallet Regulation

The savings wallet is a key differentiator and needs **regulatory care**.

---

## Why It Needs Care

- Holding customer savings **can be regulated**.
- Group savings schemes have **their own rules** in some countries.

---

## Decision: Partner with FNB

!!! note "Decision"
    YCHP partners with **First National Bank (FNB)**, which has an established framework for this wallet/savings model, and works with **FNB's compliance team** to handle legal and regulatory requirements **before launch**.
